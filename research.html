About the lab

Connecting immunity, inflammation, and the brain

The Zhu Lab at Johns Hopkins Medicine studies the immune and inflammatory mechanisms underlying neuropsychiatric conditions, with a particular focus on CNS co-morbidities such as cognitive dysfunction, depression, and sleep deficits in people living with HIV.

We investigate how chronic stress and adolescent cannabis use disrupt cognitive and mood-related brain functions. Our team also explores natural products — including constituents from Eastern herbal medicine — to develop innovative dietary supplements and pharmacological agents that support mental health.

Using advanced molecular and cellular techniques, we aim to identify novel therapeutic targets for psychiatric disorders and improve patient outcomes.

HIV & CNS Comorbidities

Investigating immune-mediated cognitive dysfunction, depression, and sleep deficits in people living with HIV.

Stress & Cannabis Effects

Examining how chronic stress and adolescent cannabis use dysregulate cognitive and mood-related brain circuitry.

Natural Products & Herbal Medicine

Developing dietary supplements and pharmacological agents from Eastern herbal medicine constituents for mental health support.

Therapeutic Target Discovery

Applying molecular and cellular techniques to identify novel targets for psychiatric disorder treatment.
